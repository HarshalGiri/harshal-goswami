
## About
I am a Director of Data & Analytics at Morgan Stanley and a PhD candidate in Artificial Intelligence. 
My work and research focus on explainable and governed AI systems for financial crime and other high-risk, regulated decision environments.

## Research Interests
Explainable AI, AI Governance, Financial Crime Analytics, Decision-Centric AI, Model Risk Management
